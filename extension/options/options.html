Tamga for chat sites

Text you paste or send on the AI chat sites listed below, and text files you attach there, are checked by your organisation's own Tamga server before they reach the site. It is sent to that server and nowhere else, and this extension does not keep it.

Settings marked "set by your administrator" come from policy and cannot be changed here.

The stored key is never shown. Leave this empty to keep it.
